Privacy Policy

ENG Flick keyboard (the "App") does not collect, store on any server, or share your personal information.

Information the App handles

The App works offline. The text you type, your clipboard history, your shortcuts, and your keyboard settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent over the internet and are never shared with the developer or any third party.

You can delete the clipboard history at any time from Settings > General, and uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.

Subscription and payments

The App offers a subscription through Google Play. Payments and subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play, and the developer does not receive or store your payment information. Google's Privacy Policy applies to these transactions.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
